LEGAL & POLICIES

TERMS & PRIVACY

Read our policies regarding bespoke allocations, insured transit, and client confidentiality.

1. Bespoke & Limited Edition Allocations

Garments released by Atelier are manufactured in strictly restricted allocations (typically 20 numbered pieces per silhouette). Once an allocation is exhausted, pieces enter our archival registry and are not re-issued in the same seasonal iteration.

2. Complimentary Shipping & Delivery

We provide complimentary insured courier delivery across all districts of Bangladesh. Orders placed before 1:00 PM BST are dispatched the same business day.

3. Returns, Fittings & Exchanges

Unworn, unaltered pieces in original condition with all fabric security tags intact may be exchanged or returned within 14 calendar days of delivery. Clients are also invited to our Banani Flagship Showroom for complimentary tailoring adjustments.

4. Client Privacy & Data Security

We treat client records with complete confidentiality. We never sell or share personal client records, measurement profiles, or payment details with third parties.
