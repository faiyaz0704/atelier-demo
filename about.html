THE MANIFESTO

ARCHITECTURE FOR THE BODY

Founded on the principle of extreme restraint. Designed for permanence.

DISCIPLINE & CRAFT

Crafted with Strict Geometric Rigor

Every piece in our collections begins as a study of geometric drape and weight. We eliminate superficial embellishments to focus solely on silhouette, proportion, and natural fiber integrity.

Each coat, jacket, and evening silhouette is finished by master tailors in our dedicated Dhaka workroom, requiring up to eighteen hours of manual precision per piece.

OUR PILLARS

THE THREE PRINCIPLES

01

Permanence Over Novelty

We reject disposable seasonal trends. Every piece is drafted with timeless proportions that maintain relevance for decades.

02

Pure Natural Fibers

Zero synthetic blends. We work exclusively with superfine Australian merino wool, organic mulberry silks, and unbleached Belgian linens.

03

Restricted Allocations

Produced in numbered editions of twenty pieces worldwide to preserve rarity, minimize waste, and ensure uncompromised quality control.
